Offscreen getDisplayMedia spike

Proves whether Design B (record everything in one offscreen doc) is viable on this machine.

  1. Open a Google Meet with a 2nd participant (or any tab playing audio, e.g. a YouTube video) in another tab — so there is remote audio to capture.
  2. Come back here, click Run capture test.
  3. A screen-picker will appear — pick a window/screen that is NOT this tab (ideally your presented window).
  4. Talk into your mic for ~6s. It records, then plays back below.
  5. Confirm: you see the picked screen, hear the remote participant, hear your mic, and there is NO echo.
