# Loom Competitor Research — June 2026

> Research to position our self-hosted Cap-fork ("Ustoz") as a better Loom. Sources cited inline.
> Generated by a background research agent on 2026-06-20.

## 1. Product & core flow
Loom = async video messaging. Acquired by **Atlassian Oct 2023 for $975M**. Core loop: install desktop/Chrome → record screen+cam → uploads in real time → instant share link → viewer watches in browser, leaves timestamped reactions/comments → creator gets view notifications.
2025–26 features: screen+cam (1080p Business, no 4K free), transcript 50+ langs, AI (auto title/summary/chapters, filler+silence removal, "Edit by Transcript"), Jira auto-ticket from bug recordings, deep Confluence/Jira/Rovo embed, CTAs, analytics.

## 2. Strengths (what users love)
- **Speed-to-share** (#1): link ready before you finish recording, no export wait.
- **Edit by Transcript**: cut video by editing text — "3x faster than timeline scrubbing".
- **Silence/filler removal**: 12-min → 9-min; ~78% filler accuracy, natural sounding.
- **Atlassian bug-to-Jira** flow (sticky for Jira shops).
- **Viewer experience**: no account needed to watch; threaded timestamped comments + emoji.
- **Transcription breadth**: 50+ languages.

## 3. Weaknesses & top complaints
- **Pricing shock (biggest):** Creator Lite (free viewer seats) **eliminated Feb 2026** → auto-converted to paid; a team of 100 went ~$240→~$24,000/yr. Co-founder publicly complained about his own invoice. **Trustpilot 1.4/5** in 2026. ([Supademo](https://supademo.com/blog/loom-pricing))
- **Free plan = trial:** 25 videos lifetime, 5-min cap, 720p, Loom branding, no download/edit/AI. ([ExtForge](https://extforge.com/blog/loom-free-plan-limits-alternatives))
- **Performance/reliability** since Atlassian migration: crashes, frozen frames, audio-sync, failed uploads, Q4-2025 outages, login loops.
- **Editing too basic:** trim/stitch only; chapters/CTAs/captions/edits stripped from downloaded MP4 (web-player only).
- **Support & billing:** charges after cancellation, refunds refused, "AI-run" support, no bulk export.
- **Privacy:** no self-hosting on standard plans; staff can access data; unclear AI processing; can't disable AI at workspace level. ([BuildBetter](https://blog.buildbetter.ai/do-they-own-your-data-loom-privacy-policy-reviewed-v2/))
- **UX confusion:** users report trouble even finding download/share.

## 4. Feature gaps (frequently requested, missing/poor)
Countdown timer; "start over" retake; playlists/folders; bulk export/API; self-hosting/BYO-S3; **translation/dubbing (none, despite 50+ lang transcription)**; offline-first recording; reliable mobile; **AI chat over transcript (none)**; good action-item extraction (~75%, misses implied); screenshot+GIF in same tool.

## 5. AI features — reality
Business+AI tier ($24/user/mo) only. Auto-title 84%; auto-chapters good >3min but weak on short videos & "mechanical"; filler removal 78%; action items ~75%; summaries = "fast first draft, not finished". Processing lag (45-min webinar = 8 min). **No AI chat/RAG, no translation/dubbing.** "Since they added AI it is faulty all the time" (G2). ([AI Brain Jet](https://aibrainjet.com/loom-ai-review/))

## 6. Pricing
| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | Free | Free | 25 videos, 5 min, 720p, branding |
| Business | $18 | $15 | Unlimited, 4K, trim — **no AI** |
| Business+AI | $24 | $20 | + AI |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | SSO/SCIM/SLA |
Gripes: Creator Lite removal (8 days notice); AI +$6 upsell; block-tier billing (51 users pay for 100); refunds refused; no bulk export on cancel (data hostage). ([Vyds](https://vyds.io/blog/loom-pricing))

## 7. Competitors (vs Loom)
- **Cap (cap.so)** — open-source, self-hostable, BYO-S3, no viewer per-seat toll, Win+Mac (our base).
- **Vidyard** — deep sales/CRM tracking; pricey ($59/user).
- **Tella** — more polished recordings, layout switching, better mobile.
- **Screen Studio** — best visual polish (auto-zoom, motion blur); one-time $229; Mac-only, no sharing infra.
- **Zight/CloudApp** — video+screenshots+GIF in one; no 5-min cap.
- **Scribe** — auto step-by-step text+screenshot guides (SOPs).

## 8. How to beat Loom → TOP 7 (impact × feasibility for our stack)
1. **Self-host as the core narrative** — privacy + cost + no data hostage. Hero copy: "your server, your data, your cost." BYO-S3 + custom domain at launch. (We have it.)
2. **AI chat over transcript (nobody has this)** — Gemini + RAG; "ask your video a question" on the watch screen. (In plan.)
3. **UZ/RU transcript + translation (language moat)** — Loom has zero translation; serve Central Asia/CIS. (New.)
4. **Viewer-free pricing** — never charge viewers; direct attack on Creator Lite disaster. (New.)
5. **Unlimited free tier (storage-gated)** — vs 25-video cap. (New.)
6. **Semantic YouTube-style chapters + Tasks → Telegram** — named chapters on any length; tasks copyable as Markdown / sent to Telegram. (Chapters/tasks planned + new TG twist.)
7. **One-click full export (ZIP) = "exit insurance"** — every video+transcript+chapters+summary; anti-lock-in. (New.)
